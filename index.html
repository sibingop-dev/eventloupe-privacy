1What Eventloupe does

Eventloupe is a Chrome DevTools extension that helps developers and marketers inspect the analytics and tracking events fired on any website they are actively debugging. It captures and displays tracking requests (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok, etc.) that are already visible in the Chrome DevTools Network tab.

2Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect any personal information about you
  • We do not transmit any data to our servers or any third-party servers
  • We do not use analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
  • We do not require an account or login
  • We do not track which websites you visit

3What the extension reads on a page

To show you tracking events, Eventloupe runs a small script on web pages that observes calls to known analytics APIs (such as dataLayer.push, gtag, fbq) and requests to known tracking endpoints, including the request body where the event details are sent. Those events may contain identifiers that the site itself sends to its analytics vendors. Eventloupe does not send them anywhere: they are shown in the DevTools panel, kept temporarily in your browser as described below, and included in an export or report only when you create one yourself.

4How captured data is stored

Tracking events captured while you use Eventloupe are stored exclusively in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage APIs:

  • chrome.storage.session — temporary in-memory storage that is automatically cleared when you close the browser or the browser session ends. Used to restore the event feed if Chrome's service worker restarts.
  • chrome.storage.local — used only to persist your personal alert rule configurations (if you create any). This data never leaves your device.

No captured tracking data is ever written to disk beyond Chrome's own storage mechanisms, and none of it is ever sent outside your browser.

5Permissions explained

Eventloupe requests the following Chrome permissions:

PermissionWhy it is needed
<all_urls> Required to inject the tracking interceptor into any website you open for inspection. Without this, the extension cannot observe tracking events on the page you are debugging.
webRequest Used to observe (not block or modify) network requests made by tracking pixels that fire before the DevTools panel is opened, such as early-loading beacons and server-side pixels.
scripting Required to inject a small JavaScript hook into the inspected page's main context to intercept dataLayer pushes, gtag() calls, fbq() calls, and other JS API-level tracking events.
tabs Used to read the current tab's URL so events can be correctly grouped by page in the Eventloupe panel and audit reports.
storage Used to save your alert rule preferences locally in your browser (see above).

6Third-party services

Eventloupe does not integrate with or communicate with any third-party service. The extension operates entirely offline once installed.

7Children's privacy

Eventloupe is a developer tool intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
